# Permafrost YouTube subtitle collection

Collected on 2026-09-29 from public videos published by the official **Toplitz Productions** YouTube channel. Video files were not downloaded.

The `.vtt` files in `original-language/` are the original-language **automatic captions** made available by YouTube. They are retained for research; each item below links to its source video. No subtitle text has been edited or treated as a source of game facts without checking the underlying video and first-party materials.
